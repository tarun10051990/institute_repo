Our Mission

CareerPath is a career guidance platform designed to help students discover their true potential through our unique 5-dimensional career assessment. We combine advanced psychometric testing with expert career guidance to help students make informed career decisions.

Our assessment framework evaluates students across Orientation Style, Interest, Personality, Aptitude, and Emotional Quotient - providing a holistic understanding of their strengths and career compatibility.
